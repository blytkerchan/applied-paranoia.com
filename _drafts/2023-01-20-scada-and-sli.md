SRE poses three basic questions: how does it break, how do I know it broke, what do I know when I know it broke. It’s a new field in IT, but I would argue it’s been core to OT since forever. The first question (how does it break) is the question you answer with a DFMEA, which is already a common practice in OT and part of power system modelling and design. The second question (how do I know it broke) is the question you answer with SLI (Service Level Indicators). In OT, this is essentially SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition).
