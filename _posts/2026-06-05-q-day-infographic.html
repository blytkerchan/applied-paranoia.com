Timeline of Key Events
2024
NIST PQC Standards Finalized
NIST Policy
FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) published after 8-year global competition. The post-quantum migration clock starts officially.
Google Willow: Below-Threshold QEC Demonstrated
Google Hardware
105-qubit Willow chip proves that adding more physical qubits actually reduces error rates — a necessary condition for fault-tolerant scaling. 1 verified logical qubit.
2025
Gidney Algorithm: RSA-2048 Cost Collapses
Algorithm
New paper cuts physical qubit estimate for breaking RSA-2048 from ~20M to under 1 million using magic state cultivation. Single largest downward revision in CRQC resource estimates.
Microsoft Majorana 1
Microsoft Hardware
First topological qubit processor announced. Parity lifetimes of 1–12 ms in Al-based devices. Topological QEC overhead ~10× lower than surface code. DARPA selects Microsoft for US2QC final phase.
IBM Fault-Tolerant Roadmap · Quantinuum 48 LQ
IBM Quantinuum
IBM targets Starling (200 LQ) by 2029 and 1,000+ LQ by early 2030s. Quantinuum Helios achieves 48 verified logical qubits with ion-trap, best encoding ratio to date.
2026 · NOW
Microsoft Majorana 2 · 20s Parity Lifetime
Microsoft Hardware
InAs–Pb tetron device achieves ~20s parity lifetime (up from 1–12 ms) — 3+ orders of magnitude improvement. Topological gap doubled to 70 µeV. Characterization only — no gates or algorithms demonstrated. Timeline accelerated to 2029.
Google/Drake/Boneh: ECC-256 Under 500K Physical Qubits
Google Algorithm
Breaking Bitcoin/TLS elliptic curve crypto shown to require fewer than 500,000 physical qubits in minutes — 20× reduction from Litinski 2023. Threat to ECC now considered nearer than RSA.
Year of Quantum Security · CNSA 2.0 Compliance Deadline Approaches
NIST Policy
Jan 2027: all new US national security systems must be CNSA 2.0-compliant by default. NIST will deprecate quantum-vulnerable algorithms in 2030, full removal by 2035. EU mandates PQC deployment starting 2026.
2027 – 2029
Expected: First Small Fault-Tolerant Algorithm Runs
IBM Microsoft Milestone
First demonstrations of fault-tolerant quantum algorithms at small scale. IBM Starling (200 LQ), Microsoft topological prototype, IonQ CRQC target for 2028. Still well below RSA-2048 threshold.
