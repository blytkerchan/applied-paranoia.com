# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this Discord bot ("Little Monarch", "this bot") collects, uses, and handles information when you interact with it.

---

## 1. Who This Applies To

This policy applies to any Discord user who interacts with the Bot in any server where it is present, or via direct message.

---

## 2. What Data the Bot Collects

The Bot may process the following data **transiently** (i.e., only for the duration of handling your request):

- **Message content** — the text of messages you send that the Bot is invoked to process.
- **Discord user IDs and usernames** — to identify who sent a message and to format responses.
- **Server and channel IDs** — to know where a message was sent.
- **Timestamps** — included automatically by Discord in every message event.

The Bot **may** persistently store any of the above in a database or log file.

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Used

Data is used to generate a response to your message. Specifically:

- Your message content is forwarded to a third-party AI inference service (see Section 5) to generate a reply.
- No data is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than producing the response.

---

## 4. Data Retention

The Bot may retain message data after a response is delivered. Conversation history may be stored between sessions.

---

## 5. Third-Party AI Services

To generate responses, the Bot forwards your message content to a third-party AI provider. Depending on configuration, this may include services such as:

- [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (Google LLC)
- [OpenAI API](https://openai.com/) (OpenAI, L.L.C.)
- [Mistral API](https://mistral.ai/) (Mistral AI)
- [Groq API](https://groq.com/) (Groq, Inc.)
- [Hugging Face Inference API](https://huggingface.co/) (Hugging Face, Inc.)
- [Together AI](https://www.together.ai/) (Together Computer, Inc.)

**Your message content is subject to the privacy policy of whichever provider is in use.** The Bot operator does not control how these providers process, log, or retain data on their end. You are encouraged to review the relevant provider's privacy policy before sharing sensitive information with the Bot.

The Bot operator makes no warranty regarding the data handling practices of any third-party AI provider.

---

## 6. Discord's Role

This Bot operates on top of the Discord platform. Discord itself collects and processes data in accordance with [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). The Bot operator has no control over Discord's data handling.

---

## 7. Data Shared With Third Parties

Beyond the AI inference provider described in Section 5, the Bot does not share your data with any third party.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

This Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are under that age, do not interact with the Bot. The Bot operator does not knowingly collect information from minors.

---

## 9. Security

The Bot is a personal or hobby project. It is provided as-is, with no guarantees of security or uptime. Do not share sensitive, confidential, or personally identifying information with the Bot.

---

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data (e.g., access, deletion, correction). If you have a concern, contact the Bot operator via the contact method below.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
